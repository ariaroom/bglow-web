Current Exhibition · Online

Life, Love & Everything She Creates

International Juried Virtual Exhibition · Women In Arts Network

August 24 – October 30, 2026 · Online / International

About the Exhibition

Life, Love & Everything She Creates brings together 50 women artists from diverse backgrounds and creative practices, exploring life, love, memory, identity, and the experiences that shape artistic creation.

The works gathered here do not offer one version of life, love, or creativity. They move between the personal and the imagined, the familiar and the unfamiliar — some artists work with subjects close to their everyday lives, while others use abstraction, symbolism, or form to express what cannot be easily described.

Exhibited Work · Selected Artist Soohyun Jo

Sanctuary in the Storm
